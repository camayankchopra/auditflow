Fixed Asset Workpaper

Upload the client's asset register. Get the roll-forward by class, depreciation recalculated per asset, additions and disposals testing, and integrity checks. Download the register template (sample data with 3 planted errors).

None

1 Fixed asset register

One row per asset, in the template format: class, dates, opening gross, additions, disposals, write-off, closing, accumulated depreciation, depreciation, NBV.

Blank = one year before year end, plus one day

2 Materiality and thresholds

Blank = performance materiality
Rounding tolerance for checks

3 Depreciation policy

Applied to every asset class as a starting point, with Companies Act Schedule II style lives. Edit per class on the Parameters tab afterwards.

4 Trial balance tie-out (optional)

Enter the amounts per trial balance / lead schedule to get an instant tie-out. You can also fill these in Excel later.

What you'll get

  • Summary - checks, depreciation result, TB tie-out
  • Roll-forward - gross, acc dep and NBV by class
  • Depn Recalc - every asset recomputed
  • Additions - key items flagged, vouching columns
  • Disposals - profit/loss and acc dep released
  • Checks - row-level exceptions highlighted

Register columns

Asset ID, Description, Asset class, Date of purchase, Date of sale / write-off, Opening gross, Additions, Disposals, Write-off, Closing gross, Opening acc dep, Depreciation for the year, Acc dep on disposals, Closing acc dep, NBV current year, NBV prior year, Sale proceeds.
Bold = required.
