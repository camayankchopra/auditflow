Contra Elimination

Journal listing in. Offsetting lines are removed, what is left is split into a debit population and a credit population ready for sampling, and the net ties back to the trial balance account by account. Download a sample journal listing and trial balance (with a reversal, a cancelled invoice and a few traps).

None

1 Journal entry listing

Journal no, date, account, narration, and either Debit and Credit columns or one Amount column. A journal number is needed for within-entry matching.

2 Trial balance (recommended)

The mapped TB (output of the TB Mapping tool works) with CY Balance and PY Balance. Used to prove that opening plus the net of the remaining lines equals the closing balance.

3 Details and rules

Evidence = reversal wording, the same reference, an identical narration or a journal number quoted. Equal and opposite amounts without evidence are kept and flagged for review.

What you'll get

  • Summary: original, eliminated and remaining lines and entries, with debit and credit amounts
  • Debit and credit populations, ready to copy into the sampling tool
  • Contra Eliminated list: every pair, why it matched, with a review column
  • TB tie-out by account: opening + remaining net = closing
  • Equal and opposite lines that were not eliminated are flagged
  • Reconciliation checks that prove nothing was lost
